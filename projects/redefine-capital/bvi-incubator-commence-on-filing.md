# BVI Incubator Fund —「遞件即營運」法規依據

用途：向投資人、銀行或不熟悉者說明「為什麼我們能這麼快上線」。Redefine Capital · 2026。

## 核心結論

BVI Incubator Fund 採「先營運、後核准（commence-on-filing / deemed）」機制：**遞交「完整」申請後 2 個工作天即可開始營運（收款、交易），無須坐等 FSC 正式核准證書**。而銀行/券商開戶更早——公司設立完成即可啟動，亦不需等 FSC。

## 法源

**Securities and Investment Business (Incubator and Approved Funds) Regulations, 2015**（2015年6月1日生效），搭配 Incubator and Approved Funds Guidelines。

## 條文機制（多家頂級 BVI 律所一致解讀，逐字引用）

> "The applicant is deemed to be an incubator or approved fund upon submission of a complete application and may commence business two business days after receipt by the Commission of the application."
> — Conyers / Ogier / Forbes Hare

> "Incubator and approved funds have the ability to commence business within two business days of lodging a complete application for approval with the Commission."
> — Harneys / Mourant / Loeb Smith

## 唯一前提：申請必須「完整」

> "If the Commission considers the application to be incomplete, it will advise the applicant within two business days of receipt of the application and will outline any further requirements. The applicant will then have seven days (subject to extension) to provide the further information, after which the application will be considered abandoned."

→ 意即：只要文件一次做對（律所的價值所在），即可 2 個工作天上線；若不完整，FSC 2 天內通知補件，7 天內補齊即可。

## 完整時間線

| 階段 | 是否需 FSC 核准？ |
|---|---|
| 公司設立完成 → 開銀行戶 + IBKR 戶 | 否（僅需公司設立） |
| 遞交完整 FSC 申請 → 2 個工作天後可營運（收款、交易） | 否（遞件即可營運） |
| FSC 背景審核 → 正式核准證書 | 行政確認，不影響已開始的營運 |
